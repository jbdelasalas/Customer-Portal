# Applying for an Art Fresh Trade Account

A guide for customers applying online.

**Apply here:** <https://customer-portal-nine-delta.vercel.app>

---

## What you will need

Have these ready before you start — it takes about 15 minutes with everything
to hand.

**Documents** (photo or scan is fine, as long as it is readable):

- Certificate of Registration / Articles of Incorporation / DTI registration
- BIR Certificate of Registration
- Business Permit
- Valid ID of the authorised signatory

**Optional but helpful:** Specimen Signature Card, Company Profile, Board
Resolution, Secretary's Certificate, latest General Information Sheet, recent
audited financial statement.

**Photos** — taken with your phone during the application:

- A photo of yourself (like a 2x2 ID photo)
- A photo of your business premises, showing your signage

**Information:**

- Business name as registered, and TIN
- Your business and delivery addresses
- Names of the people authorised to place orders, receive goods, and handle
  payment
- Bank details, if you are applying for credit

Use a **phone** if you can. The photos and the signature are easier there.

---

## Step 1 — Create your account

**Apply for a Trade Account** → enter your name, email, and a password of at
least 10 characters.

Use an email you check. Everything about your application goes to this address.

---

## Step 2 — Fill in the form

Ten sections, matching the paper Customer Information Sheet.

**Your answers save automatically as you type.** You can close the page and come
back — signing in again returns you to where you left off.

Fields marked with a red asterisk are required.

A few worth explaining:

- **Collection address** — where your business is based.
- **Delivery address** — where orders should go. Tick the box if it is the same.
- **Google Maps link** — optional, but it genuinely helps our drivers find you.
  In Google Maps, find your location, tap **Share**, and paste the link.
- **Authorised signatories** — the people who may act for your business. One
  person can hold all three roles; put the same name in each.
- **Payment arrangement** — choosing **Credit** reveals the credit fields. What
  you request is subject to approval; the terms you are granted may differ.

---

## Step 3 — Take your photos

Two are required. Tap **Open camera**, then **Take photo**.

- **Your photo** — face the camera in good light. This is checked against
  your ID, so make sure your face is clear.
- **Your establishment** — the storefront or entrance, with signage visible.
- **Inside your establishment** — optional, but it helps us understand your
  business.

If your camera will not open, or you are on a computer without one, use
**Upload instead**. Your phone will offer its camera anyway.

You can retake any photo before submitting.

---

## Step 4 — Upload your documents

One slot per document. **Choose file**, pick the photo or PDF.

Each accepts images or PDF, up to 10 MB (20 MB for the longer documents). If a
file is rejected, it is usually too large or in an unexpected format — take a
fresh photo rather than scanning at maximum quality.

Uploading again to the same slot replaces what was there.

---

## Step 5 — Sign the declaration

Read the declaration, then sign in the box with your finger, stylus or mouse.

Enter the **name and position of the authorised signatory** in the section
above the box first — you cannot confirm the signature without them.

Not happy with it? **Clear and sign again**.

Then **Confirm signature**. Your signature is recorded with the date, time, and
the exact wording you agreed to. It is legally binding.

---

## Step 6 — Submit

**Submit application**.

If anything is missing, the page lists exactly what — fix those and submit
again. Once accepted you get a reference number like `APP-2026-000012`. Keep it.

---

## After you submit

You can sign in at any time to see your application's status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** | Received, waiting to be reviewed |
| **Under review** | Someone is looking at it |
| **Information requested** | We need something more — sign in to see what, edit, and resubmit |
| **Approved** | Your account is open |
| **Rejected** | We could not approve it. The reason is shown |

> **Please note:** email notifications are not switched on yet. **Sign in to
> check your status**, or contact us — we will not be able to email you when
> your application progresses.

**If you are asked for more information**, sign in, open your application, make
the change, and submit again. It returns to the queue.

**Once approved**, you receive a customer code (like `AFCC-000002`). You may
also be asked to have your Customer Information Sheet notarised — we will print
it and send it to you.

---

## If you cannot sign in

**Forgot your password?** Email-based reset is not available yet. **Call us**
and we can issue you a temporary password over the phone. Have your customer
code or business name ready so we can confirm who you are.

You will be asked to choose your own password as soon as you sign in with the
temporary one.

**Account locked?** Five wrong passwords locks it for 15 minutes. Wait, then
try again.

---

## Questions we are often asked

**Can I finish this later?** Yes. Everything saves as you type. Sign in again
to continue.

**Can someone else finish it for me?** Only from your account, since it is
signed with your login and your signature.

**Can I change something after submitting?** Not directly. Contact us and we
can send it back to you for editing.

**Do I have to use my phone?** No, but the photos and signature are easier on
one. A computer works if it has a camera, or you can upload photos taken
separately.

**Is my information safe?** Your documents are stored privately and are only
visible to you and our reviewing staff. Nothing is publicly accessible.
